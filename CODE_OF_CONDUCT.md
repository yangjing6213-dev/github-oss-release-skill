# Project Code of Conduct

Version 1.0

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Protect credentials, personal data, and confidential reports.
- Respect licenses, attribution, platform rules, and authorization boundaries.
- Help keep discussions focused on reproducible project work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information or security details without permission.
- Spam, fake engagement, credential solicitation, or instructions to bypass safety controls.
- Knowingly submitting unlicensed or malicious material.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation
that violate this policy. Report conduct concerns through a private repository
contact when available; do not expose sensitive evidence publicly. Enforcement
decisions should be documented proportionately and avoid unnecessary personal data.
